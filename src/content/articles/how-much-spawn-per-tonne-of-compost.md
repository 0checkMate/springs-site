Published guidance for button mushrooms generally puts the spawning rate at about **0.5 to 1% of the wet weight of the compost**, which is roughly **5 to 10 kg of spawn per tonne**. The right figure for your farm depends on the strain, the type of spawn and the condition of your compost, so confirm it with your supplier before you order.

## The usual range

| Spawning rate | Spawn per tonne of compost | Spawn per 100 kg of compost |
|---|---|---|
| 0.5% | 5 kg | 0.5 kg |
| 0.75% | 7.5 kg | 0.75 kg |
| 1% | 10 kg | 1 kg |

Sources differ slightly. Some grower guidance gives 0.5 to 0.75%, while research and strain data from mushroom institutes use up to 1% of wet compost weight. Treat 0.5 to 1% as the working range and let your supplier's strain-specific advice decide where in it to sit.

## From kilograms to litres

Spawn is often sold by the litre, but rates are quoted by weight. One grower source puts a litre of grain spawn at about 600 g, which would make 5 to 10 kg of spawn roughly 8 to 17 litres per tonne of compost. Spawn density varies with grain and moisture.

## A worked example

You have 3 tonnes of wet compost and your supplier recommends 0.75%.

3 tonnes × 7.5 kg per tonne = **22.5 kg of spawn**.

Always weigh or measure your compost, because estimating by eye is the most common reason growers over- or under-order.

## What happens with more or less spawn?

- **More spawn** can speed up colonisation, because there are more starting points for the mycelium. Some growers report that the extra spawn does not raise the final yield, so it adds cost without a guaranteed return.
- **Less spawn** may slow colonisation, which leaves the compost open to competing organisms for longer.

## Spawning methods

How you place the spawn matters as much as how much you use.

- **Mixed spawning:** spawn is mixed evenly through all the compost. It is generally regarded as the most uniform method.
- **Surface spawning:** spawn is spread over the top and mixed into the top 3 to 5 cm.
- **Layer spawning:** spawn is placed in three to four layers within the compost, then covered with a thin layer of compost.

## Before you spawn

- The compost must be free of ammonia smell and cooled to a suitable temperature. See our guide on [button mushroom compost preparation](/grow/button-mushroom-compost-preparation/).
- Bring stored spawn up to room temperature first. See [how to store mushroom spawn](/grow/how-to-store-mushroom-spawn/).
- Use only healthy spawn. See [how to select good button mushroom spawn](/grow/how-to-select-good-button-mushroom-spawn/).

## Common mistakes

- Estimating compost weight instead of measuring it.
- Mixing unevenly, which leaves bare patches that colonise slowly.
- Adding spawn to compost that is still warm or still smells of ammonia.
- Opening more packs than you will use in one session.
